# Credit Analysis

## Objective

Determine whether the platform can meet its financial obligations under base and downside conditions.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Synthesizes asset quality, cash flow, financing terms and sponsor considerations from a lender's perspective.

**Builds on:** All preceding analytical sections and financial-model outputs.

**Supports:** Risk analysis and investment committee recommendation.

---

- [ ] Credit weaknesses

- [ ] Debt-service metrics
- [ ] Liquidity analysis
- [ ] Structural protection assessment
- [ ] Counterparty considerations
- [ ] Indicative credit conclusion

---

## Key Questions

- What is the primary source of debt repayment?
- How resilient is debt service under downside conditions?
- What protections prevent value leakage?
- What could cause default or restructuring?
- Which conditions would be required for credit approval?

# Market and Regulation

## Objective

Assess the French power-market, regulatory and revenue-support environment affecting the platform.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Explains the external conditions that determine revenue stability, growth opportunities and downside exposure.

**Builds on:** Government, regulator, grid-operator and market publications.

**Supports:** Financial model, credit analysis and risk analysis.

---

## Deliverables

- [ ] French electricity-market overview
- [ ] Renewable support mechanisms
- [ ] PPA and merchant-market context
- [ ] Permitting and grid-access considerations
- [ ] Relevant policy and regulatory risks
- [ ] Market-price and curtailment considerations

## Key Questions

- How are the assets remunerated?
- How exposed are revenues to merchant electricity prices?
- Which regulatory mechanisms protect or constrain cash flow?
- What policy changes could affect the platform?
- What market assumptions will eventually enter the financial model?

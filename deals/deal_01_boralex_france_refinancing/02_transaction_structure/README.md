# Transaction Structure

## Objective

Explain the legal, ownership and cash-flow perimeter of the platform financing.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Defines exactly what sits inside the transaction and how the relevant entities interact.

**Builds on:** Deal fact sheet, corporate disclosures and legal-entity information.

**Supports:** Financing structure, credit analysis and risk analysis.

---

## Deliverables

The analytical output for this workstream is documented in [`deliverable.md`](./deliverable.md).

- [ ] Pre-transaction structure
- [ ] Post-transaction structure
- [ ] Borrower and guarantor perimeter
- [ ] Ownership diagram
- [ ] Cash-flow and distribution pathways
- [ ] Refinancing mechanics
- [ ] Known and inferred structural features

---

## Key Questions

- Who is the legal borrower?
- Which entities and assets are inside the financing perimeter?
- How do cash flows move through the structure?
- What changed from the previous multi-borrower structure?
- Where might structural subordination or leakage arise?

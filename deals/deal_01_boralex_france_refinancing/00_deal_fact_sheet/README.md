# Deal Fact Sheet

## Objective

Establish a single, reliable source of verified facts describing the transaction.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Provides the factual baseline used by every subsequent chapter.

**Builds on:** Public announcements and primary-source documents.

**Supports:** All subsequent sections.

---

## Deliverables

- [ ] Transaction summary
- [ ] Announcement and closing dates
- [ ] Borrower and ownership structure
- [ ] Sponsors and principal stakeholders
- [ ] Financing amount and stated purpose
- [ ] Assets and activities included in the financing
- [ ] List of verified and unverified transaction terms

---

## Key Questions

- What transaction occurred?
- Who are the borrower, sponsors, lenders and advisers?
- What is the financing amount and purpose?
- Which assets and activities are included?
- Which facts are confirmed, inferred or unavailable?

# Risk Analysis

## Objective

Identify, allocate and evaluate the material risks affecting the transaction and determine whether mitigants reduce them to acceptable levels.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Makes explicit what could go wrong, who bears each risk and how the transaction responds.

**Builds on:** Transaction, sponsor, asset, market, technical, financing and credit analyses.

**Supports:** Sensitivity analysis and investment committee recommendation.

---

## Deliverables

The analytical output for this workstream is documented in [`deliverable.md`](./deliverable.md).

- [ ] Risk register
- [ ] Cause-and-consequence analysis
- [ ] Probability and impact assessment
- [ ] Contractual and structural mitigants
- [ ] Residual-risk assessment
- [ ] Risk owner and allocation
- [ ] Early-warning indicators

---

## Key Questions

- What are the principal sources of downside?
- Who bears each risk?
- How could each risk affect cash flow and debt service?
- Which mitigants are contractual and which are assumptions?
- Which residual risks remain material?

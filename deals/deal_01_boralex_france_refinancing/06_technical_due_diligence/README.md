# Technical Due Diligence

## Objective

Evaluate whether the assets can operate safely and reliably at the production, cost and availability levels assumed.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Tests whether the physical asset base can generate the forecast cash flows.

**Builds on:** Asset information, technical literature and clearly disclosed proxy assumptions.

**Supports:** Financial model, credit analysis and risk analysis.

---

## Deliverables

The analytical output for this workstream is documented in [`deliverable.md`](./deliverable.md).

- [ ] Technology overview
- [ ] Resource and production considerations
- [ ] Asset age and remaining useful life
- [ ] Availability and degradation considerations
- [ ] Operations and maintenance arrangements
- [ ] Construction and development risks
- [ ] Insurance and major technical-risk considerations

---

## Key Questions

- Are the production assumptions technically credible?
- What could reduce availability or output?
- Are operating-cost assumptions realistic?
- Do asset lives support the proposed debt tenor?
- Which technical issues require specialist verification?

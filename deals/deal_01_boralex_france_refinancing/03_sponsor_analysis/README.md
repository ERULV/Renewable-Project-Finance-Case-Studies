# Sponsor Analysis

## Objective

Assess the sponsors' ownership, financial capacity, strategic incentives, operating record and willingness to support the platform.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Separates sponsor strength and behaviour from the standalone quality of the financed assets.

**Builds on:** Annual reports, investor materials and sponsor disclosures.

**Supports:** Credit analysis, risk analysis and investment committee recommendation.

---

## Deliverables

The analytical output for this workstream is documented in [`deliverable.md`](./deliverable.md).

- [ ] Boralex company profile
- [ ] Energy Infrastructure Partners profile
- [ ] Ownership and governance analysis
- [ ] Financial capacity assessment
- [ ] Renewable operating and development track record
- [ ] Strategic alignment
- [ ] Potential forms and limits of sponsor support

---

## Key Questions

- Who ultimately owns and controls the platform?
- What relevant experience do the sponsors possess?
- How financially capable are they?
- What incentives do they have to support the transaction?
- Is support contractual, reputational or merely assumed?

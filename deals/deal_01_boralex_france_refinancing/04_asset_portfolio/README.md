# Asset Portfolio

## Objective

Describe the operating assets, development projects and portfolio characteristics supporting the financing.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Identifies the physical and commercial sources of cash flow available to service debt.

**Builds on:** Company portfolio disclosures, project databases and regulatory information.

**Supports:** Technical due diligence, financial model and credit analysis.

---

## Deliverables

- [ ] Portfolio inventory
- [ ] Technology and geographic breakdown
- [ ] Operating versus development-stage capacity
- [ ] Ownership interests
- [ ] Commercial operation dates where available
- [ ] Revenue-contract profile
- [ ] Portfolio concentration and diversification analysis

---

## Key Questions

- Which assets are included?
- How much capacity is operating, under construction or in development?
- How diversified is the portfolio?
- What contracts or regulatory mechanisms support revenue?
- Which assets contribute most to risk and cash flow?

# Financing Structure

## Objective

Explain how the platform is financed, why the structure was selected and how it balances flexibility with lender protection.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Connects the platform's cash-generating capacity with the debt instruments and contractual protections used in the transaction.

**Builds on:** Transaction structure, public financing disclosures and model outputs.

**Supports:** Credit analysis, risk analysis and investment committee materials.

---

## Deliverables

- [ ] Facilities and financing purpose
- [ ] Sources and uses where estimable
- [ ] Debt amount and currency
- [ ] Tenor and amortisation analysis
- [ ] Pricing and interest-rate considerations
- [ ] Security and guarantee framework
- [ ] Covenants, reserves and cash controls
- [ ] Hedging considerations

---

## Key Questions

- Why was a single platform financing chosen?
- How might the debt amount have been sized?
- How and when is debt expected to be repaid?
- What flexibility does the structure provide to the sponsors?
- How are lenders protected against downside risk and cash leakage?

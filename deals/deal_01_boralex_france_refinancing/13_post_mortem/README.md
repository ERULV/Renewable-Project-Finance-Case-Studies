# Post-Mortem

## Objective

Evaluate the transaction critically, document limitations and extract lessons that can be transferred to future investment decisions.

## Role in the Transaction Workflow

Converts one completed case study into durable professional knowledge.

**Builds on:** The completed case study and its documented limitations.

**Supports:** Framework development and future case studies.

---

## Deliverables

The analytical output for this workstream is documented in [`deliverable.md`](./deliverable.md).

- [ ] Transaction rationale assessment
- [ ] Alternative-structure discussion
- [ ] Benefits achieved by sponsors
- [ ] Risks accepted by lenders
- [ ] Public-information and modelling limitations
- [ ] What could have been structured differently
- [ ] Transferable lessons
- [ ] Future research priorities

---

## Key Questions

- Why was this structure selected over credible alternatives?
- Who gained the most flexibility or protection?
- Which assumptions were most uncertain?
- Would the transaction remain attractive under different market conditions?
- What principles should be reused in the next case study?
